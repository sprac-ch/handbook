# Glossary

Abbreviations and terms used across the handbook. Explained once, spelled the same everywhere.

- Index: Jump to a letter

## Terms

| Term | Full name | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Com Recr | Commando recruitment | Runs the recruitment days where your fitness and aptitude are tested and your army function is assigned. |
| CTF | Capture the Flag | Security competition where you solve hacking challenges to find hidden "flags". A popular way to practise cyber skills. |
| Cy Bat 42 | Cyber Bataillon 42 | Militia cyber battalion of the Kommando Cyber, active since 1 January 2022. Most members come from the CyLG. |
| CyLG | Cyber Lehrgang | 40-week cyber training of the Swiss Armed Forces at Jassbach barracks. You apply in the first week of the RS and finish as sergeant. |
| Cyber-Militia | | Umbrella term for the army's cyber militia path: the CyLG and service in Cy Bat 42. |
| DDPS | Federal Department of Defence, Civil Protection and Sport | The federal department the Swiss Armed Forces belong to. VBS in German, DDPS in English. |
| DD | Continuous service soldier | Someone who completes the whole military service in one go instead of RS plus yearly refresher courses. |
| Kdo Cy | Kommando Cyber | Army command responsible for cyber defence, electronic warfare and ICT. Cy Bat 42 belongs to it. |
| Militia | Militia system | Citizens serve part-time instead of as professional soldiers. Most of the Swiss Armed Forces, including Cy Bat 42, are militia. |
| NCO school | Non-commissioned officer school | School for future non-commissioned officers (NCOs), after the RS. |
| Recruitment | | A few days of tests before the RS that decide if and where you serve. |
| Refresher Course | | Refresher course after the RS, usually about 3 weeks per year. |
| RS | Recruit school | Basic military training. The first block of service for everyone who is fit for service. |
| SPARC | | Free, self-paced cyber pre-training of the Swiss Armed Forces for young people before the RS. Levels: Explorer, Defender and Operator. |
| Sergeant | | Non-commissioned officer rank. CyLG graduates finish with this rank. |
